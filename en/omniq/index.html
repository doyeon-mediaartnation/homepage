How it works

Four steps is all it takes

  1. 1

    Install

    Get OMNIQ from the App Store or Google Play. Guests get one free try per device.

  2. 2

    Pick a theme

    Choose the theme to apply. Before the first recording you're told what is sent where, and asked for consent.

  3. 3

    Record & transform

    Press record, one token is used and the video is transformed in real time while you shoot.

  4. 4

    Save & share

    Keep the result on your device and share it to Instagram or any other service.

Tokens

Try for free, then transform with tokens

One token per AI transformation. New accounts get free tokens, and if a transformation fails on our side after it starts the token is refunded automatically. Tokens are bought through App Store and Google Play in-app purchase.

  • 3 free tries (1 per device + 2 on sign-up)
  • 1 token = 1 transformation · 15-second clip
  • Full refund if nothing is produced

Privacy & safety

OMNIQ you can trust

Nothing stays on our servers

Frames and audio sent during a transformation are used only to produce the result and deleted the moment the session ends.

Explicit consent first

Before the first recording we explain what is transmitted and to whom, and ask for consent. Nothing is sent until you agree.

No biometric analysis

No face detection, landmark extraction or any other biometric processing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is OMNIQ?

An iOS and Android app developed and operated by Media Art Nation. Record a short clip with your camera and the theme you chose is applied in real time; keep the result on your device or share it to other services.

Is it available in my country?

OMNIQ is on the App Store and Google Play in 10 languages, including Southeast Asian and Latin American markets. Pricing follows each store's local catalogue.

Are my videos stored on your servers?

No. Results are stored only on your device. Data sent during a transformation is used only for that transformation and deleted when the session ends.

Can you build a theme for our brand or IP?

Yes. Themes are built from reference images and prompts and can ship instantly without an app update — seasonal, brand or whole-universe themes, and we are preparing on-site activations for events and pop-ups. Get in touch.
